# Code of conduct

This code covers project discussions, issues, reviews and other project-managed collaboration. It creates no response-time commitment or public-launch announcement.

Treat participants respectfully. Critique evidence and work without personal attacks. Welcome questions and differences in experience. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others, publish private information or pressure participants to carry out unsafe manufacturing work. Do not conceal defects, fabricate inspection or origin evidence, or present unreviewed output as professional approval.
