# Production-requirement formula

Record demand, policy reserve, usable finished quantity, and committed receipts for one product and period. Use `net need = demand + policy reserve - usable finished quantity - committed receipts`, then apply a supplied yield basis only when its definition and unit support it.

Finished quantity on hold is not usable without an explicit authority. Preserve a negative result as surplus or apply the stated policy; do not silently clamp, release, or update inventory.
