# Standard-work draft checklist

Capture the approved sequence, observed task elements and times, observer/date, time units, takt definition and source, allowed WIP, quality checks, operator feedback, and review owners. Do not turn an observed time into a target without owner approval.

If takt or cycle evidence is missing or incompatible, keep the standard-work draft conditional and route the calculation or definition gap to its owner. The draft is not an effective instruction.
