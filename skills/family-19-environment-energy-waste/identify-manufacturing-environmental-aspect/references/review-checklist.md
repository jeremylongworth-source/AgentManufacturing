# Inventory environmental aspects

- Use one row per activity/flow/operating condition; the same flow may have different impacts under different conditions.
- An aspect is a process interaction, such as a discharge; an impact is a possible environmental change. Record that relationship without asserting the impact occurred.
- Unknown composition stays unknown. A stream name, safety data sheet for a feedstock, or existing disposal label alone does not establish the output stream's classification.

## Source boundary

Record source ID, date, applicable scope, revision, claim location, and verification status. No source is assumed applicable from its title alone. For AM-25's bounded catalogue and government-guidance checks, see the repository [source evidence](../../../../docs/development/AM-25-source-evidence.md). Package methods remain usable with supplied evidence if this repository-level context is absent; a dependent source claim must await verification.
