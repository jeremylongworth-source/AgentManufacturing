# Jurisdiction and safety

Generic manufacturing methods do not establish Canadian legal applicability. Establish the activity, location, jurisdiction, current source version and qualified reviewer for the particular conclusion.

The [Canadian jurisdiction model](https://github.com/jeremylongworth-source/AgentManufacturing/blob/main/docs/architecture/canadian-jurisdiction-model.md) and [source registry](https://github.com/jeremylongworth-source/AgentManufacturing/blob/main/docs/architecture/canadian-source-registry.json) identify routing questions and evidence. A jurisdiction flag asks for assessment; it is not a legal determination. Verify current official sources before operational reliance.

## Review boundaries

Skills support drafts, calculations, evidence gaps and handoffs. They do not authorize machine operation, isolation or restart, safety-circuit changes, engineering signoff, product release or legal conclusions. Do not fabricate records or bypass safeguards.

Read the [safety boundary model](https://github.com/jeremylongworth-source/AgentManufacturing/blob/main/docs/architecture/safety-boundary-model.md) and [scope boundaries](https://github.com/jeremylongworth-source/AgentManufacturing/blob/main/docs/architecture/scope-boundaries.md). Missing authoritative evidence must remain visible even if unrelated generic arithmetic can proceed.

## Standards and confidential material

A citation does not grant permission to reproduce protected standards. Use authorized access and precise source locators. Do not submit confidential plant records or sensitive vulnerability details to public issues. [[License and Support|License-and-Support]] gives the private reporting route.
