# Nonconformance triage checklist

Capture the observed defect, detection method, product/process boundary, lot or time window, evidence source, and any immediate hold or escalation already recorded. Mark every unverified item as unknown. A missing affected quantity is not zero, and a sample count is not a lot count.

The triage output should state a provisional priority only when the supplied evidence supports it, explain the rationale, list the next owner, and identify whether containment, engineering, safety, or regulatory review is needed. It must not infer root cause or final disposition.

Review owner: quality. If supplied evidence indicates an immediate safety or regulatory concern, escalate to the qualified owner and retain the evidence record. This checklist does not authorize a hold, release, customer notice, or process change.
