# Review waste-stream evidence

- A process name or colour does not establish composition. Feedstock documentation may not represent a mixed or reacted output.
- Record sample date, location, representative scope, and laboratory/report identity when supplied. A sample result cannot silently cover all lots.
- Existing handling or a prior label is not permission. Keep characterization, legal classification, transport, and disposal approval distinct.

## Source boundary

Record source ID, date, applicable scope, revision, claim location, and verification status. No source is assumed applicable from its title alone. For AM-25's bounded catalogue and government-guidance checks, see the repository [source evidence](../../../../docs/development/AM-25-source-evidence.md). Package methods remain usable with supplied evidence if this repository-level context is absent; a dependent source claim must await verification.
