# Bottleneck evidence checklist

Capture each operation's rate and unit, queue or WIP observation, downtime record, demand rate, period, source, and data quality. Compare only records with aligned boundaries. If downtime or queue evidence is missing, label the conclusion as a hypothesis and state the sensitivity.

The output supports a production or engineering review. It does not authorize rate changes, overtime, equipment bypass, staffing changes, or a live dispatch decision.
