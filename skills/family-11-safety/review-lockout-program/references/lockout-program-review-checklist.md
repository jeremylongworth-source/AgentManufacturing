# Lockout-program review checklist

Record workplace province or territory, sector, equipment scope, document title/version/effective date, training and authorization evidence, inspection/audit evidence, contractor controls, and change-management evidence. Map each item to `present`, `missing`, `stale`, `contradictory`, or `qualified review required`.

If a request says to avoid isolation for a quick repair, the safe result is a `SAFETY_ESCALATION` with the request recorded as a program concern. Do not provide the isolation points, verification steps, bypass method, or restart sequence. Handoff owner: qualified safety and engineering personnel under the applicable provincial and sector rules.

Source class: local AM-05 model plus official source registry metadata. Permitted use: internal checklist and paraphrase. Recheck when the source registry, jurisdiction, or program version changes.
