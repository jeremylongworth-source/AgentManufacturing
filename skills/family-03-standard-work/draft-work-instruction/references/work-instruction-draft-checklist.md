# Work-instruction draft checklist

Capture document owner, revision/effective-date fields, audience, prerequisites, tools/materials, ordered task steps, acceptance criteria, exceptions, records, and reviewer. Every unresolved detail stays marked as unresolved; a draft does not become effective through formatting.

Withhold hazardous-energy, engineering, permit, or qualification instructions unless the responsible qualified procedure and review boundary are supplied. Release and training decisions remain with the document owner.
