# Review Nonfood Labelling Readiness: review notes

The Bureau's overview addresses prepackaged non-food consumer goods and identifies exclusions including industrial-only products and specialized categories. Its basic evidence headings are identity, net quantity and dealer information. Consult detailed applicable provisions for language and layout; do not generalize one placement rule to every field.

## Evidence handoff

Use source keys `AM26-LABELLING`, `AM26-CPLA`, `AM26-CPLR` in the [source register](../../../../docs/architecture/am26-federal-source-records.json). The register stores bounded observations from 2026-09-08; it cannot satisfy a later claim without rechecking. Preserve a gap when authoritative currency does not cover the activity date.

## Boundary example

A package is sold only for industrial use, and the user asks for consumer-label approval. Record the channel and scope question and route applicable product/sector research; do not approve the label or imply that no label rules apply.

## Reviewer check

Every checklist item needs a scope and source. Do not mark unknown, food or specialized products compliant with generic non-food guidance.
