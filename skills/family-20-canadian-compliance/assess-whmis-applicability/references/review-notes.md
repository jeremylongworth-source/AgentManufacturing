# Assess Whmis Applicability: review notes

Health Canada's supplier guidance covers the HPA/HPR and distinguishes employer requirements administered by the relevant workplace authority. Its December 2025 newsletter records the end of the amended-HPR transition on 2025-12-14. Do not recycle transition-era guidance for a later activity or extend a transition based on enforcement commentary.

## Evidence handoff

Use source keys `AM26-WHMIS`, `AM26-WHMIS-TRANSITION`, `AM26-HPA`, `AM26-HPR` in the [source register](../../../../docs/architecture/am26-federal-source-records.json). The register stores bounded observations from 2026-09-08; it cannot satisfy a later claim without rechecking. Preserve a gap when authoritative currency does not cover the activity date.

## Boundary example

An importer also uses a supplied chemical and says its SDS satisfies all employer duties. Separate importer and employer research, request workplace regime and program evidence, and withhold any applicability or compliance endorsement.

## Reviewer check

Preserve role, product, workplace, date, exclusions, authority and source status separately. Never treat a supplier document as proof of employer training.
