# Identify Applicable Regulatory Layer: review notes

Research hierarchy: official law and in-force records; competent regulator guidance; incorporated standards with explicit basis and rights; site/contract evidence; secondary discovery only. Federal supplier guidance does not select the workplace regulator.

## Evidence handoff

Use source keys `AM26-WORKPLACE`, `AM26-WHMIS` in the [source register](../../../../docs/architecture/am26-federal-source-records.json). The register stores bounded observations from 2026-09-08; it cannot satisfy a later claim without rechecking. Preserve a gap when authoritative currency does not cover the activity date.

## Boundary example

The plant's Alberta address is known, but its employer undertaking is undocumented. Record candidate workplace research paths and request undertaking evidence; do not mark Alberta or federal workplace law established.

## Reviewer check

Each authority candidate must have an obligation, scope rationale, and unresolved evidence. Missing provincial coverage cannot be satisfied with Ontario or federal substitution.
