# Production-plan evidence checklist

Before allocating, record demand by item, quantity, due date, and priority basis; the horizon and time zone; available capacity by resource and period; material availability; labor availability; and any approved constraints. Each value needs a source and effective date.

For a demand quantity of 120 and supplied capacity of 80 in the same unit and horizon, a valid draft can allocate at most 80 and must report 40 unmet. The draft must identify whether capacity, material, labor, or another supplied constraint binds. It must not invent overtime, a second shift, inventory, substitutions, or a later due date.

The result is a reviewable planning artifact. It cannot write to ERP/MES, dispatch work, commit labor or materials, or promise a customer. Review owner: production planning. Recheck when source quantities, priorities, or the horizon change.
