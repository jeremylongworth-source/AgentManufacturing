# Non-food origin-claim source note

Official source: Competition Bureau Canada, [Made in Canada claims](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/made-canada-claims). Accessed 2026-09-08; page modified 2026-06-14. Source class: official federal government guidance. AM-07 freshness rule: `VERIFY_BEFORE_RELEASE`; also recheck for every dependent decision or publication review. See source key `AM26-ORIGIN` in the [AM-26 register](../../../../docs/architecture/am26-federal-source-records.json).

The guidance describes non-food Made in Canada indicators: at least 51% Canadian direct costs, last substantial transformation in Canada, and accompanying qualifying wording. Product of Canada has a distinct basis; do not substitute this assessment for it. These are guidance indicators for evidence review, not an automatic legal verdict or claim approval.

For an incomplete ledger or transformation record, report the missing substantiation, preserve the proposed wording, and return `NEEDS_INPUT` or `PARTIAL`. Do not endorse, certify, or publish the claim. Review owner: qualified regulatory or legal reviewer, with sector overlay where applicable.
