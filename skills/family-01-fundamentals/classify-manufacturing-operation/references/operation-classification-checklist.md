# Operation classification checklist

Record the requested activity, input and output material, transformation or assembly step, unit/batch/continuous context, and any adjacent storage or transfer. Classify the operation boundary only; do not classify the whole employer or infer a legal code.

If the evidence describes receiving, storage, movement, or dispatch without transformation, route the adjacent work to logistics. If it describes transformation, assembly, blending, or finishing, retain the manufacturing route while recording any unresolved context.
