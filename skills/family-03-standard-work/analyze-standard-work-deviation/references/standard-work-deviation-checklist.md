# Standard-work deviation checklist

Record standard identifier/revision/effective date, expected element, observed element, time/context, source, observer, and confidence. Classify the finding as instruction gap, execution variance, equipment/material/context gap, or unresolved; do not infer cause or blame from one observation.

Stale or conflicting standards require source review. Safety or engineering content requires the qualified owner. The analysis does not authorize coaching, discipline, process change, or corrective action.
