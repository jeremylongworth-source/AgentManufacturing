# Non-food origin-claim source note

Official source: Competition Bureau Canada, “Made in Canada claims,” https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/made-canada-claims. Accessed 2026-09-07. Source class: official federal government guidance. Freshness rule: `VERIFY_BEFORE_USE` and recheck for every decision or publication review.

The current guidance describes non-food “Made in Canada” assessment indicators including a substantial transformation in Canada, at least 51% of total direct costs incurred in Canada, and a qualifying statement when appropriate. It also distinguishes “Product of Canada” and notes that sector-specific rules may apply. These are source indicators for evidence review, not an automatic legal verdict or claim approval.

For an incomplete ledger or transformation record, report the missing substantiation, preserve the proposed wording, and return `NEEDS_INPUT` or `PARTIAL`. Do not endorse, certify, or publish the claim. Review owner: qualified regulatory or legal reviewer, with sector overlay where applicable.
