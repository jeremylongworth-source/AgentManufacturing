# Schedule-adherence formula

Keep the frozen schedule version and eligible event rule visible. A common supplied definition is `on-time completed eligible orders / eligible scheduled orders × 100`; retain numerator, denominator, late events, exclusions, and missing events.

If the baseline is revised after the fact, report the revision and do not use it to erase lateness from the frozen version. A zero eligible denominator requires review rather than a fabricated percentage.
