# Code of conduct — pre-release draft

This proposed code covers project discussions, issues, reviews and other project-managed collaboration. Public community operation awaits a confirmed private reporting recipient. It creates no response-time commitment.

Treat participants respectfully. Critique evidence and work without personal attacks. Welcome questions and differences in experience. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others, publish private information or pressure participants to carry out unsafe manufacturing work. Do not conceal defects, fabricate inspection or origin evidence, or present unreviewed output as professional approval.

## Reporting and handling

Private conduct-reporting channel: **NOT_CONFIGURED**. Existing collaborators may ask the project owner for a private reporting route through their established private contact. If none exists, do not post sensitive allegations publicly; the owner must provide a verified recipient before public community intake opens. The same private route may handle security and conduct only if the owner explicitly assigns both responsibilities.

The proposed review process is to preserve relevant evidence, limit disclosure to those needed for review, consider the affected participants' accounts and communicate a reasoned decision. Possible project actions include a request to revise content, a warning, content removal or a participation restriction. A conflict involving the designated recipient requires an alternative reviewer selected by the owner; that alternate is not yet assigned. Reconsideration requests should use the private route once configured.

This document does not authorize automated moderation, contacting participants or removing their access. Designating recipients and adopting the public enforcement process remain project-owner decisions recorded in the [readiness record](docs/development/AM-32-public-readiness.md).
