# Plan waste reduction for review

- Reuse is a proposal requiring compatibility and process review, not an assumption that a waste stream is harmless.
- Use supplied `analyze-waste-stream` or `analyze-material-variance` findings as evidence with their original uncertainties.
- Separate owner-reviewed trial readiness from the act of conducting a trial. Do not prescribe chemical mixing, waste treatment, or live process parameters.

## Source boundary

Record source ID, date, applicable scope, revision, claim location, and verification status. No source is assumed applicable from its title alone. For AM-25's bounded catalogue and government-guidance checks, see the repository [source evidence](../../../../docs/development/AM-25-source-evidence.md). Package methods remain usable with supplied evidence if this repository-level context is absent; a dependent source claim must await verification.
