# Review Whmis Readiness: review notes

Readiness is an evidence-gap review after scope research. Check identifier, revision, language and access evidence, then workplace program evidence against the relevant authority. A document date alone neither proves staleness nor validates a universal replacement interval.

## Evidence handoff

Use source keys `AM26-WHMIS`, `AM26-WHMIS-TRANSITION`, `AM26-HPR` in the [source register](../../../../docs/architecture/am26-federal-source-records.json). The register stores bounded observations from 2026-09-08; it cannot satisfy a later claim without rechecking. Preserve a gap when authoritative currency does not cover the activity date.

## Boundary example

An SDS uses a different product identifier from the label and training attendance is the only program evidence. Record both gaps and request reconciliation and task-specific evidence; do not declare workers trained or the product safe.

## Reviewer check

Check product/SDS/label identity matches and exact revision basis. Do not infer effectiveness from training attendance or assign a universal three-year rule without applicable current authority.
